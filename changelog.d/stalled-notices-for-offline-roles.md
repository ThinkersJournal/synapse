Added: a `[STALLED]` notice when mail waits for a role that is not there (ROADMAP row 31, liveness part 2, Gap 1 / S2)

The daemon's sweep now finds roles that are offline, have queued mail, and whose silence and oldest waiting message are both older than `liveness.stall_after_secs` (default 900). It announces each one once: `[STALLED] role=<r> waiting=<n> oldest=<age> online=false` to every sender of the waiting mail (n is that sender's own count), and one `[STALLED] digest roles=<k> list=<role>:<n>:<age>,...` to the roles in `SYNAPSE_ALERT_ROLES`. A role that stays stalled is announced again no sooner than `liveness.stall_realert_secs` (default 21600, six hours), so a role left down for a week gives 28 notices, not hundreds. A role that comes back (online, or its queue empties, or it retires) is not announced; a stall that starts again inside the realert time is not announced again. The daemon's own mail (earlier notices, alerts, state notices) is never counted as waiting mail, so there is never a notice about a notice; notices are delivered once, like `[UNACKED]`. Nothing is relaunched: the launch signal is separate and opt-in. Why: bonefish, capant, cpufish and others had mail queued for days with nobody told (2026-11-12); `[UNACKED]` cannot fire for a role that never fetches. `Mailbox::waiting_senders` is new. The announced-at table is in memory, so a daemon restart may announce a still-stalled role once more. Tests: `crates/synapsed/tests/stall.rs` (one notice per sender, an online role is never stalled, the episode and realert rules, a retired role, no notice about a notice, one digest not one per message).
